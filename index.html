How AutoPool Navigates Indian Dealership & RTO Rules

Designed specifically for Indian motor vehicle laws, dealer franchise territories, and RTO tax structures.

📍

City & RTO Territory Clustering

Car manufacturers penalize dealerships if they sell cars to buyers outside their assigned geographical territory. AutoPool pools buyers by regional cluster (e.g. Delhi-NCR, MMR Mumbai, Bengaluru) so the dealer can invoice legally and deliver via official showroom channels.

⚖️

All-India BH-Series Support

If you have a central government job or work at a private company with offices in 4+ states, you qualify for Bharat (BH) series plates. BH pools allow buyers across different Indian states to pool together for any dealer offering the deepest pan-India discount.

🛡️

Refundable ₹5,000 Escrow Pledge

To prevent fake buyers from wasting the pool's time, every member locks a ₹5,000 refundable commitment. If the dealer reverse auction doesn't hit the target minimum discount threshold (e.g., ₹60,000+), you can withdraw 100% of your deposit with zero penalties.

🔍

Independent PDI (Pre-Delivery Inspection)

Buying in bulk doesn't mean accepting old stock or showroom-damaged cars. AutoPool mandates a 150-point PDI check (VIN verification for month/year of manufacture) before the final disbursement of funds to the dealer.
